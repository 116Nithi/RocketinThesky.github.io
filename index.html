About Me

Hi, this is who I am.

Hi, I'm Nithi Thipprasoet, a student in the Faculty of Industrial Education and Technology, majoring in Computer Technology at King Mongkut's Institute of Technology Ladkrabang (KMITL). I was born and raised in Somo Khaek, Phitsanulok.

Outside of class, I enjoy gaming, watching movies/series, listening to Hip-hop/R&B, and I'm into cars.
